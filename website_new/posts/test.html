Understanding AdamW: Worst-Case Convergence Analysis Strikes Back

November 25, 2026
\newcommand{\R}{\mathbb{R}}

Introduction

Before the invention of Muon [JJB+24], AdamW [LH19] has been arguably the most popular optimizer for large-scale deep learning. Its fast and stable convergence made it a default choice in most practical scenarios.

Unfortunately, there is no satisfying theoretical explanation for AdamW's success. In particular, the original Adam paper [KB15] contains a mistake in the convergence analysis, and other works [LRJ23] are unable to explain the advantages of AdamW over simple SGD variants. Some researchers even suggest that neural networks could have evolved to make AdamW the best optimizer. In this work, we will try to address this issue.
